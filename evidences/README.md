## Methodological Clarifications & Historical Runs

1. **Superseded 200-Sample Exploration:**
   - An earlier exploratory benchmark run on 200 samples (recorded in `data/evaluation_results/` showing 94% accuracy, 100% precision, 88% recall, 0% FPR) was generated during preliminary testing prior to final model integration.
   - That exploratory run is superseded by the controlled 100-sample balanced benchmark committed in this directory (`evidences/03_evaluation_report.txt`), executed on the final evaluated build (commit `89e4948`).

2. **Label Leakage in Synthetic Authentication Flags:**
   - In `test_dataset.csv`, synthetic authentication attributes were constructed such that all 50 phishing samples had `spfPass=False` and `dkimPass=False`, while all 50 legitimate samples had `spfPass=True` and `dkimPass=True`.
   - The heuristic scoring engine adds +25 for SPF failure and +20 for DKIM failure, introducing label leakage that inflates primary benchmark accuracy to 93.00% (recall 86.00%).
   - To provide full academic honesty, the benchmark was also executed under **Neutral Authentication Controls** (`spfPass=True`, `dkimPass=True` for all 100 samples), removing authentication penalties entirely. Under this control, accuracy measured **83.00%** (recall 66.00%, precision 100.00%, F1 79.52%, FPR 0.00%; TP=33, TN=50, FP=0, FN=17).
   - Both results are reported transparently in the Capstone paper and this evidence folder.

3. **Active Subsystems in Offline Evaluation:**
   - **Layer 1 (Active):** Heuristic rules + Typosquatting + Tabular Ensemble (RF + XGBoost).
   - **Layer 2 (Active):** Pre-trained DistilBERT ONNX checkpoint (`lemonade-sdk/phishing-email-detection-distilbert-ONNX`, Hugging Face, Apache-2.0, evaluated without author fine-tuning).
   - **Layer 3 (Neutralized):** Defaulted to neutral constant score $S_{behavioral} = 15$ due to absence of longitudinal client interaction telemetry.
   - **Layer 4 (Skipped):** VirusTotal network lookups were bypassed in offline mode.
