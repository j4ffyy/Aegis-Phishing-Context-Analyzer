# Participant Information Sheet & Informed Consent Form

**Study Title:** Usability and Comprehension Evaluation of Explainable AI (XAI) Interfaces for Enterprise Email Security  
**Principal Investigators:** Aegis Research Team, Capstone 2 Project  
**Institution:** School of Information Technology / Computer Science  
**Target Participants:** Administrative, Finance, Customer Support, and Retail Operations Personnel  

---

## 1. Purpose of the Study
You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating the clarity, understandability, and usability of an AI-powered email security tool called **Aegis**. We are studying how different visual explanations of email security warnings affect non-technical staff understanding and decision-making. We are evaluating the **software**, not your personal skill or knowledge.

## 2. Description of Participation
If you choose to participate, you will:
1. Review 6 realistic office email scenarios on a desktop computer.
2. View the Aegis security alert overlay for each email.
3. Briefly explain in your own words what the security alert indicates.
4. Choose what action you would take (e.g., open link, verify with colleague, report as suspicious).
5. Complete a 10-question usability questionnaire regarding your experience.
The entire session will take approximately **25 to 30 minutes**.

## 3. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to answer specific questions, take a break at any time, or withdraw from the study without penalty or impact on your employment.

## 4. Risks and Benefits
- **Risks:** The risks are minimal and no greater than normal computer office work. You will encounter mock phishing emails, but no real malicious software or live links are active.
- **Benefits:** Your feedback will directly contribute to improving security tools designed for Philippine SMEs to protect against cyber threats.

## 5. Data Privacy and Confidentiality
All data collected during this study will be kept strictly confidential in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012) and international research ethics standards:
- Your identity will be anonymized using an alphanumeric code (e.g., `P01`, `P02`).
- No personal names, company names, or identifying contact information will be included in published research papers or public repositories.
- Evaluation data will be stored securely on password-protected and encrypted drives accessible only by the research team.
